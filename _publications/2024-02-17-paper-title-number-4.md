This paper presents a fused least-squares method based on a phase-locked loop for software-based resolver demodulation and error correction. The proposed approach combines phase-locked-loop signal tracking with least-squares-based ellipse fitting to improve the accuracy of resolver signal processing.

The paper was presented at the **2026 IEEE 9th International Electrical and Energy Conference (CIEEC)**.
