## About Me

Hi, I’m **Hao WU (吴昊)**, and you can also call me **Quentin**. I was born in 2000 and have an **ENFP** personality type.

I received my bachelor’s degree from the **Beijing Institute of Technology** in June 2024, specializing in **Mechanical and Electronic Engineering with a focus on Robotics**.

After graduation, I worked for six months as a **Research Technical Assistant** in the [AIMS Research Group](https://sites.google.com/view/hailong-huang/home), led by Professor Hailong Huang in the Department of Aeronautical and Aviation Engineering at **The Hong Kong Polytechnic University**. My research focused on the collaborative delivery of goods using **unmanned aerial vehicles and unmanned ground vehicles**.

I later joined the research group of [Academician Dayong Jin](https://www.eitech.edu.cn/?tid=119&p=teacher) at **Eastern Institute of Technology, Ningbo**, where I worked as an engineer conducting research on **organoid intelligence**.

I am currently pursuing an **MRes degree in Electrical and Electronic Engineering** at the **University of Nottingham Ningbo China**, under the supervision of [Dr. Shuo Wang](https://research.nottingham.edu.cn/en/persons/shuo-wang/). My research interests include:

* Robotics
* Embodied Intelligence

## Ancient Chinese Coins

Beyond my academic work, I am also one of the leading experts and collectors of ancient Chinese coins in mainland China. I began studying ancient Chinese coins at the age of six and have spent many years developing my expertise in coin authentication, classification, and historical research.

Over the years, I have served as a chief consultant or authenticator for several leading Chinese coin grading and auction companies, including:

* [GBCA (Gongbo)](https://www.gongbocoins.com/)
* [Zhizhen Grading](https://www.baocuicoin.com/view/4.html)
* [Yuequanwan Antiques Auction House](https://wwj.beijing.gov.cn/bjww/362771/362775/325842777/index.html)

I have also engaged in limited coin trading to support my studies and research. In recent years, my main focus has been the collection and study of Qing Dynasty coin prototypes, including:

* **Diaomu (雕母):** hand-engraved master coins used to produce mother coins
* **Muqian (母钱):** mother coins used in the production of circulating coins
* **Yangqian (样钱):** pattern or sample coins produced for evaluation and approval

I am always happy to communicate and exchange ideas with fellow collectors, researchers, and anyone interested in ancient Chinese numismatics.
